# Privacy Policy for WYPE Security App

**Effective Date:** [Date]

## Overview

WYPE Security ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the WYPE Security mobile application ("App").

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide:
- **Emergency Contact Information:** Phone numbers of emergency contacts you designate
- **Voice Recordings:** Custom wake phrases you record for emergency activation
- **Google Account Information:** When you choose to enable backup functionality

### Information Automatically Collected:
- **Location Data:** GPS coordinates when emergency is triggered (used only for emergency SMS)
- **Device Information:** Android version, device model (for compatibility purposes)

## How We Use Your Information

### Emergency Response:
- Send SMS alerts to your designated emergency contacts
- Include your location in emergency messages
- Trigger automatic device wipe for security protection

### Backup Services:
- Store emergency contact information in your personal Google Drive
- Create backup files before emergency device wipe

### App Functionality:
- Monitor for wake phrase detection
- Maintain background service for continuous protection

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage:
- All voice recordings and contact information are stored locally on your device
- Data is encrypted using Android's built-in security features

### Cloud Storage:
- Backup data is stored in your personal Google Drive account
- You control access through Google's authentication system

### No Third-Party Sharing:
- We do not share your personal information with third parties
- We do not have access to your Google Drive data

## Permissions Used

The app requires the following permissions for functionality:

- **Microphone:** To listen for wake phrases
- **SMS:** To send emergency messages
- **Location:** To include location in emergency messages
- **Contacts:** To access emergency contact information
- **Google Drive:** To backup data (optional)
- **Device Admin:** To perform factory reset in emergencies

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Delete your account and all associated data
- Disable backup functionality at any time
- Modify or remove emergency contacts
- Revoke Google Drive access

## Data Retention

- Local data is deleted during emergency device wipe
- Backup data remains in your Google Drive until you delete it
- You can manually delete backup files at any time

## Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app.
